International Delegate Visa Support

Bridging the distance between heritage and innovation. We've streamlined the Ghana E-Visa process to ensure a seamless journey to Tech-Islamic Fest 2026.

Ghana's E-Visa Process

Follow this four-step digital application guide for international delegates traveling to Accra.

info

Processing time is usually 3-5 business days. We recommend applying at least 3 weeks before departure.

1

Digital Portal Entry

Access the official Ghana Immigration Service portal and create a secure account using your email address and travel passport details.

Visit Official Portal open_in_new
2

Form Submission

Complete the online application form. Ensure your name matches your passport exactly. Select 'Conference' or 'Business' as the visit type.

3

Digital Payment

Process the non-refundable visa application fee through the secure payment gateway. All major international credit cards and mobile wallets are accepted.

4

Approval Notification

Once approved, you will receive a digital E-Visa certificate. Download and print this document to present upon arrival at Kotoka International Airport.

Required Documentation

Please prepare digital copies of these essential documents before starting your application.

Password

Valid Passport

Passport must be valid for at least six months from the date of arrival in Ghana with at least two blank pages.

vaccines

Health Docs

Valid Yellow Fever Vaccination Certificate is mandatory for all travelers.

photo_camera

Photo

Recent color passport-size photo with a white background taken within 6 months.

description

Invitation Letter

A formal letter from the Tech-Islamic Fusion Festival organizing committee confirming your registration and participation.

flight_land

Travel Itinerary

Copy of your round-trip flight booking and confirmed hotel reservation for the duration of the event.
