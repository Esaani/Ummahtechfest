Ethics in AI: A Maqasid approach to Generative Models

Session Description

This session explores the profound intersections between Islamic legal theory—specifically the Maqasid al-Shari’ah (Objectives of Law)—and the contemporary challenges of AI alignment. As generative models become ubiquitous, we investigate how traditional frameworks of preserving life, intellect, and wealth can provide a robust ethical foundation for modern AI safety protocols and algorithmic accountability.

What to expect

  • check_circle

    Mapping the 5 pillars of Maqasid to current AI safety research frameworks.

  • check_circle

    Case studies on biased datasets through the lens of communal justice (Adl).

  • check_circle

    Practical guidelines for developers building ethically-grounded Large Language Models.
